Etymology and derivation


[BLOCK 11: TEXT]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The word ‘Pitta’ is derived from Sanskrit root “tap”.[Su. Sa. Sutra Sthana 21/5]

[BLOCK 15: TEXT]

The root word ‘tap’ has three meaning: 1) tap dahe means to burn, 2) tap santape means heat generator and 3) tap aishwarye means able to procure eight types of attainments of a yogi (animadi aishwarye).

Pitta dosha and triguna

[REFERENCES / LINKS]
triguna : https://www.carakasamhitaonline.com/index.php?title=Triguna


[BLOCK 39: P]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
All the living & nonliving things originate from panchamahabhuta which originate from trigunatmaka prakriti as described in evolution process (utpatti krama). Dosha are evolved from panchamahbhuta , so these dosha bear triguna property. Pitta is evolved from agni that has sattva and raja predominance. [ 2 ] [Su. Sa.Sharira Sthana 1/20] Sharangadhara and Bhavaprakasha opines that pitta has sattva predominance. [Sha.Sa.Pratham Khanda 5] [ 6 ] [Bh. P. Purva Khand 3/103] [ 7 ] [Figure 01]

Sites of pitta


[BLOCK 47: P]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Dosha are present in every part of the body . [Cha.Sa. Sutra Sthana 20/9] General location of pitta dosha is the middle part of the body , marked between the heart( hridaya ) and umbilicus( nabhi ).[A.S. Sutra Sthana 1/4] [ 5 ] Pitta is predominately present in body parts between the large intestine(pakvashaya) and stomach( amashaya ). [Su. Sa.Sutra Sthana 21/6] [ 2 ] The specific seats of pitta are (Figure 02):

Among all these sites, duodenum ( amashaya ) and umbilicus (nabhi) are the prime sites of pitta.[Cha.Sa. Sutra Sthana 20/8][K.S. Sutra Sthana 27/11] [ 9 ] Amashaya (stomach) is located in between the breast (stana) and umbilicus( nabhi ). In amashaya , the digestion of all types of food takes place. [Cha.Sa. Vimana Sthana 2/17] Duodenum is the area of the digestive tract where maximum digestive process occurs. Acharya Chakrapani clears that the location of pitta is lower part of amashaya ( i.e. duodenum). [Chakrapani on Cha.Sa. Sutra Sthana 20/8]

The characteristics of pitta are:

Diagnostic and assessment tools


[BLOCK 300: TEXT]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Pitta dosha can be assessed by its normal or abnormal functionality through subjective methods like questionnaires and objective methods. Currently, there are no validated and standardized tools for


[BLOCK 301: TEXT]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
dosha


[BLOCK 302: TEXT]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
assessment within the


[BLOCK 303: TEXT]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
body


[BLOCK 304: TEXT]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
. Pitta vriddhi and kshaya features can be assessed through a subjective questionnaire. Ayurvedic physicians assess the normalcy and abnormality through the ayurvedic principles of examination (pariksha).


[BLOCK 305: TEXT]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Subjective and objective methods which may be used to assess the normal functioning of pitta dosha are:


[BLOCK 306: UL]
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Test for assessment for digestive enzymes and hormones like serum lipase [ 11 ] , serum amylase [ 11 ] , serum pepsinogen [ 12 ] and serum gastrin [ 13 ] etc.
2. Hemoglobin level, Red blood cell count, Mean Corpuscular Hemoglobin, Mean corpuscular hemoglobin concentration. [ 14 ]
3. Subjective assessment of Intelligence quotient like Wechsler Intelligence Scale for Children (WISC) [ 15 ] .
4. Subjective assessment of emotional intelligence like Mayer-Salovey-Caruso emotional intelligence test [ 16 ] .
5. Cognitive assessment-Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA) [ 17 ] , Mini-Cog [ 18 ] & ICMR-PNT(Picture naming test) [ 19 ] etc.
6. Memory test through memory drum [ 20 ] .
7. Visual acuity-Snellen’s chart [ 21 ] .
8. Perception of vision- tachistoscope [ 22 ] .
9. Skin test like sebum content, trans-epidermal water loss and melanin content through various instruments [ 23 ] .
10. Assessment of body Temperature.
11. Subjective assessment of Agni-Self-Assessment Tool to Estimate Agnibala [ 24 ] & CCRAS Agni assessment tool( http://www.ccras.res.in/ccras_sas/ ).
